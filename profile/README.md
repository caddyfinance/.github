# Caddy Finance: The Servicing Layer for Onchain Yield

**Every new onchain yield product creates a back office nobody built. Caddy turns it into a verified institutional position adapter.**

Custodians hold the keys and staking providers execute transactions, but institutional operators still have to reconstruct the financial position: who owns it, what it has earned, when it matures, how rewards are allocated, why records disagree, and what evidence accounting systems and auditors can accept.

Caddy models, tracks, investigates, verifies, and reports each position across blockchains, custody records, client instructions, and commercial agreements. The verified record is delivered into the custody, accounting, and administration systems an institution already uses.

> **Caddy never holds client funds or signs a transaction.** Agents investigate and propose; deterministic code verifies financial results; humans retain approval authority.

## How Caddy Works

1. **Model** — Convert protocol mechanics into a position adapter covering states, entitlements, lifecycle events, and edge cases.
2. **Track** — Follow ownership, principal, rewards, maturity, and exceptions across onchain and offchain sources.
3. **Investigate** — Use read-only agents to collect evidence and propose explanations for discrepancies.
4. **Verify** — Recompute balances, rewards, cutoffs, and accounting outputs with deterministic code.
5. **Deliver** — Send a source-linked position record and evidence pack into the operator's existing workflow through an API, CSV, or system integration.

## Built for Existing Financial Workflows

Caddy is designed for operations and product teams at custody, staking, and asset platforms launching new onchain yield products for institutional accounts.

Caddy delivers the partner's testnet-to-production position build into the systems it already uses. When the partner relies on the adapter in production or requests a second protocol, the engagement converts into a maintained production-adapter relationship.

The same model can later support other Bitcoin-native protocols, liquid-staking and restaking positions, basis trades, and structured vaults—without requiring Caddy to custody assets, replace the operator's systems, or manufacture the underlying financial product.

## Control Model

* **Read-only by design** — No private keys, transaction signing, or asset movement.
* **Deterministic verification** — No financial figure ships solely on a model's output.
* **Human financial authority** — Consequential treatments are accepted, rejected, or escalated by an authorized operator.
* **Existing execution controls** — Custody and accounting systems retain their native approvals and posting workflows.
* **Complete evidence trail** — Source records, calculations, proposals, and approvals remain linked.
